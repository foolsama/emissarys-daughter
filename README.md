**Alenawe **is a newly posted field agent, political analyst, and trained mage. She is not in Skyrim because she wants to become an adventurer. She is here for her first posting after education. Then something walked out of Helgen that could not be comfortably explained, and an opportunity presented itself.

Allowing her to travel with you simply gives her a better view.

She begins formal, guarded, with a believable background job. She has her own loyalties, ambitions, conclusions, and limits. She may come to respect you. She may become your friend. An optional romance and marriage path exists - but none of those outcomes are guaranteed, and she will not abandon her judgment merely because the player is important.


## Features:



- A fully voiced custom follower with her own character arc.
- Three smaller conversational missions and two larger narrative quests.
- Reactions to major questlines, factions, locations, combat, and patterns in player behavior.
- A hidden relationship system with no approval pop-ups or visible affinity meter.
- A separate stability system that considers whether continued proximity to the player is actually survivable.
- Conversations that change in depth and tone as the relationship develops.
- Multiple stable outcomes, including professional association, friendship, optional romance and marriage...* or permanent departure.*
- Custom behavior while she is not actively following, without replacing your preferred follower-management tools.
- An optional RaceMenu preset for players who would like to use her appearance.


## Story Preview

Alenawe has been trained to believe that good institutions are slow, exact, and necessary. Skyrim is none of those things.
Yet its crude systems repeatedly identify capable people, survive impossible crises, and grant authority to individuals who have never been properly classified. Most troublingly, the Dragonborn continues to solve problems without waiting for permission - and people continue to follow.

Her reports begin as an attempt to make that pattern legible.

The pattern does not cooperate.

Her personal story concerns institutional loyalty, conditional worth, and what happens when a person trained to be exceptional begins to suspect she was never meant to become an exception. **The player influences the conditions under which she changes, but does not simply select her fate from a dialogue menu.**


## Character Notes

Alenawe is controlled, not emotionless.
She is intensely curious, politically literate, dryly funny, and accustomed to treating uncertainty as a problem to be classified. She respects competence, but competence alone will not earn her trust. She notices restraint, consistency, needless disruption, and whether the player's stated principles resemble their actual behavior.
She is not a conventional morality companion. Joining a criminal faction will not automatically offend her (although she will have *opinions*, and flagrant disregard for the social contract will put her off), and behaving heroically will not automatically reassure her. She is more interested in whether you exercise power deliberately - and in how much damage the world sustains whenever you act.
She is also not secretly waiting to become agreeable. Greater trust gives the player access to more of her reasoning; it does not replace her worldview with theirs.

## Dialogue and Relationship System

Conversations with Alenawe are intended as roleplaying choices rather than exhaustive dialogue menus.
When a conversation presents several responses, you generally choose *only one*. The unused responses may disappear for that stage of the relationship (they may return later). There is no expectation that the player exhaust every branch in a single playthrough, and there is not always one objectively “correct” answer.
Curiosity, precision, and directness tend to keep her engaged. Dismissal, intellectual condescension, or attempts to use her vulnerability against her will have consequences. That said, **you are strongly encouraged to answer in-character rather than trying to reverse-engineer an approval score.**

There is no on-screen notification when her opinion changes. Instead, watch for:

- Longer or more specific answers.
- Questions she did not previously ask.
- References to earlier conversations.
- Changes in timing, phrasing, and willingness to admit uncertainty.
- A colder, more operational register if she begins to consider you dangerous.

**A player may earn her professional respect without ever becoming her friend. A close relationship can also cool if the player's later behavior convinces her that proximity is no longer safe. She remembers what happened; she simply stops granting the same access.**
And yes: if the player repeatedly crosses clearly stated boundaries, she can leave permanently.**

## Finding and Recruiting Alenawe

Alenawe and her supervising agent, Alonythil, start in the Falkreath Jarl's Longhouse (or Inn, if it's late).

**She is** ***present *before she is willing to join you*.* If you approach before becoming relevant to her assignment, she will politely dismiss you. *This is intentional.***

Once you have met one of the recruitment conditions, find her (she will be moving around, check local governmental centers) and speak with her again. There is currently no quest-marker notification announcing that you have become eligible.

**Do not use the console or a follower framework to force-recruit her early. Doing so may bypass introductory scenes, quest initialization, or relationship tracking.**

**Some main-quest events substantially alter the context of her dialogue. They are not intended to be mandatory prerequisites for completing her personal arc.**

## Follower Management

The Emissary's Daughter includes custom AI packages, but these primarily govern Alenawe's behavior when she is *not *actively serving as the player's follower.
**This mod deliberately does not include another full teleport, outfit, home-assignment, or follower-tracking interface.** Recruit her through her normal introductory sequence first; after recruitment, she is intended to work with the follower-management system you already prefer.
[Nether's Follower Framework](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/55653): Recruit Alenawe normally, then import her into NFF. Do not force-recruit or import her before completing her introduction.
[Simple Follower Framework:](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/174017)Compatible (No special handling needed, but keep in mind this framework does not have teleports, outfits, home assignments, etc - and *neither does Alenawe*).
**Other frameworks:** *Untested*
Frameworks that replace dialogue, aggressively override quest packages, or alter follower factions may require compatibility settings. Please report reproducible conflicts rather than assuming every feature of every framework can be supported.

## Romance and Marriage

Romance is *optional *and is **not **the default purpose of the mod.
It requires a genuinely close relationship, sustained evidence that the player is safe to remain near, and respect for Alenawe's agency during her personal story. Flirting alone will not produce it, and completing enough quests will not cause it automatically.
**A non-romantic playthrough retains the complete character and narrative arc.
