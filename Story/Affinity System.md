# Affinity System

Affinity is not about virtue.
Affinity is about volatility.

## Stability Tiers
Player demonstrates dangerous volatility → Stability down.
Player endorses uncontained personal power → Stability down.
Player penetrates something she believed she controlled → Stability down.
Player makes her model stop predicting the interaction → Stability down.

### 3. Stable Asset (positive)
Requires demonstrated restraint in moments where domination was available.
Triggered when:
– Volatility low over extended playtime
– Discretion consistently high
– Institutional friction moderate or strategically aligned
– No recent chaotic spikes

You don’t reach her inner circle by being powerful and efficient.
You reach it by choosing not to escalate when escalation was easy.
Examples that would matter to her:

– You spare a defeated opponent when killing would have been trivial.
– You refuse to destabilize a city for quick gain.
– You resolve a conflict through institutional channels rather than brute force.
– You decline certain Daedric power offers.

Not because those are morally pure — but because they show you are not addicted to power acquisition.
That’s the difference between “controlled tyrant” and “disciplined sovereign.”

What unlocks:
– Internal monologues in dialogue
– Confessional lines
– Romance flag possible
– She shares operational vulnerabilities

### 2. Unknown Variable (neutral) - Default state


### 1. Strategic Liability (negative)
Triggered when:
– Sustained high volatility
– Repeated jail
- Repeated guard killings
- Vampire/Were predation (even if not caught)
– High unpaid bounty
– Randomized violence in cities
– Betrayals of institutional alignment (depending on her values)

What happens:
– Tone sharpens: Measured fear, controlled distance.
– Dialogue shortens
– She stops offering insight
– Eventually she withdraws (“ghosts”) if threshold crossed

Track behavior categories:
Category	        Tracked Actions
Violence	        Kills, aggression toward non-hostiles, locational vampire/werewolf transformation detection
Manipulation	    Bribes, intimidation, persuasion
Accumulation	    Gold threshold, properties, artifacts
;Curiosity/Knowledge	Books read, locations discovered, quest types completed

Crime bursts vs isolated incidents
Time between major power acquisitions
Whether bounties are resolved immediately or left hanging
Frequency of skill increases across categories (breadth vs narrow brute focus)
;Book interaction count (you already know when a book is read)

Three hidden values:

VolatilityScore

DiscretionScore

InstitutionalFrictionScore

VolatilityScore increases when:
– Murder crime flagged
– Assault crime flagged
– Kill non-hostile actor
– Kill surrendering actor (harder to detect, but fleeing combat state is proxy)
– Large bounty spikes

DiscretionScore increases when:
– Crime committed but not reported (hard to detect directly, but low bounty despite combat events is proxy)
– Bounty paid quickly
– No collateral kills in cities
– No bounty accumulation over long playtime

InstitutionalFrictionScore increases when:
– factions joined/changed
– Large unpaid bounty
– Jail time
– Attack guards

Chaos Tier is not directly tied to crime.

It’s tied to volatility minus discretion.

A vampire lord who kills efficiently in dungeons and leaves no witnesses?
Low chaos.
Thieves Guild stealth infiltration that avoids destabilizing cities?
Fine.

A “hero” who constantly causes city-wide brawls and pays 40k in bounty fines?
High chaos.
Guard barracks feeding farm?
Not fine.

**Main quest completions should be strong positive stability signals.**
*Saving the world is promoting system stability.*

A brute thug who:

– Kills indiscriminately
– Causes city chaos
– Leaves unpaid bounty
– Slaughters guards

Should not receive Tier 1 revelations or vilnerability.

That’s character integrity.

But a power-accumulating stealth assassin who:

– Avoids collateral
– Saves the world
– Pays fines
– Keeps cities functioning

Should absolutely reach Affinity Tier 4.

**State transitions must be slow. Do not let a single event flip tiers.**

- Stability should degrade only from repeated patterns, not isolated events.
- Dialogue handling of her vulnerability should be weighted heavily.
- Affinity Tier 4 should require:
	- Affinity ≥ 81
	- Stability = Stable Asset
	- MinQ2 passed with agency-respecting response

Use rolling averages.

Example:
ChaosTier updates only if the player remains beyond a threshold for X in-game hours or Y location changes.
That prevents “I accidentally hit a chicken, now she hates me.”

conditional openness might require:

– Sustained low volatility

She opens up not because you’re moral, but because you are controlled and thinking.

*Even at max affinity, she would still distrust concentrated personal power.*

If she’s about systems, then your fully ascended Dragonborn is her worst-case philosophical nightmare:

A single individual who can:
– Absorb dragon souls
– Command armies
– Subvert factions
– Drain institutions
– Rewrite succession crises

That is exactly the kind of figure history collapses around.

She does not care about moral cleanliness.
She cares about whether proximity to you is survivable - and whether your existence strengthens or erodes structure.

Romance should not remove that tension, the system should reflect that tension structurally. 
She trusts you enough to be vulnerable, but never enough to stop watching you.

Highest tier isn't full emotional surrender - it's "I trust you, but I will never stop preparing for you." (Batman)

Affinity shouldn’t unlock softness.

It should unlock access.

Romance becomes:

You are invited into her strategic thinking.

That is intimacy for her.

Not tearful confession.

And crucially:

If the player crosses volatility thresholds (guard draining, institutional sabotage), Tier 3 should not simply drop to Tier 2. She doesn't forget what you mean to her, or what's happened.

It should introduce:

“Active contingency mode.”

Dialogue shifts to subtle distance.
Operational questions increase.
Tone cools.
She begins talking about “necessary safeguards.”


### **Affinity Tier 0 (0-20) — Professional Association**

**(Default after recruitment)**

This is where she starts.

- She follows you because:
    - you are competent,
    - you are relevant,
    - her mentor approves,
    - it serves her mission.
- She is polite, distant, observant.
- She does **not** trust you.
- She does **not** dislike you.
- She does **not** confide in you.
- She is still “being evaluated.”

This is the **pack-mule / Lydia-adjacent floor**.

If a player never engages further, the mod is still complete here.

---

### **Affinity Tier 1 (21-40) — Respect**

“She takes you seriously.”

Unlocked by:

- supportive or curious dialogue choices.

At this level:

- Her tone warms slightly.
- She offers analysis instead of surface observations.
- She begins to comment on *why* you do things.
- She starts watching your decisions, not just outcomes.

Still no vulnerability.

Still no romance.

This is **professional respect**, not liking.

---

### **Affinity Tier 2 (41- 60) — Trust**

**Requires:**
- Low sustained volatility (Stability Tier >= 2)

“She believes you are safe to think around.”

This is the **true gateway**.

Unlocked by:

- consistency of worldview (not morality),
- discretion,
- not exploiting her position,
- showing restraint or purpose,
- engaging with her ideas rather than dismissing them.

At this level:

- She admits doubts.
- She questions Thalmor assumptions out loud.
- She asks the player questions.
- She reacts more strongly to your choices.

This is where she *starts* to like you as a person — but cautiously.

---

### **Affinity Tier 3 (61-80) — Emotional Opening**

**Requires:**
- No sustained volatility spikes, Stability Tier >= 2

“She lets herself be seen.”

Unlocked by:

- handling her vulnerability well,
- not weaponizing her doubts,
- aligning with her *principles* (not necessarily goodness),
- demonstrating conviction with a code.

Tier 3 should be far more influenced by dialogue behavior than systemic telemetry.

At this level:

- She shares personal history.
- Mentor/mother pressure becomes explicit.
- She admits fear, anger, envy, or shame.
- She may express attraction (if enabled).
- “Good time” intimacy (fade-to-black) can exist here.

This is the dangerous tier.

She can still retreat if mishandled - but still feel "complete". Tier 4 should be "bonus content" not withheld content.

---

### **Affinity Tier 4 (81-100) — Commitment / Self-Determination**

**Requires:**
- Stablity Tier 1 (Restraint under opportunity)
- Support of personal agency in mod Quests
- Supportive and/or curious + some flirty choices in mod dialogue.
- Not choosing dialogue choice 3 or 5 in MinM2

It's not about being law-abiding, it's about not becoming the sole solution.

“She chooses you as part of her future.”

At this level:

- She breaks from Thalmor expectations.
- Marriage (if you include it) is only possible here.
- Or: she chooses independence but remains allied.

This is the *end state*.

---

## What Affinity is **not**

Just to be explicit:

- It is **not** a morality meter.
- It is **not** a “be nice” counter.
- It is **not** tied directly to MQ stages.
- It is **not** required for basic follower utility.
- It is **not visible to the player.**

It measures:

> “How much of herself she is willing to entrust to you.”

# State Interactions
Dark Brotherhood does not auto-lock Tier 3 or 4.
Killing guards does.
We're not punishing crime, we're punishing systemic destabilization.

Werewolf rampages in wilderness?
Low institutional friction.

Werewolf rampages in Whiterun marketplace?
High volatility.

### Stabilty state
Driven by telemetry: guard killing, volatility, institutional friction, discretion, restraint.
1. Strategic Liability
2. Controlled Unknown
3. Stable Asset

Major Stabilizers:
– MQ completion
– Dawnguard resolved
– Dragonborn resolved
– Civil War resolved
– Major Daedric threats contained
- Mage Guild Completion

Minor Stabilizers:
– Thane status
– Paying bounty
– Low crime rate
– Skill breadth (competence)
- other guild completions

Destabilizers:
– Guard murder
– High bounty
– Jail
– Repeated assault
- specific Daedric acceptance/bargains
- specific "evil" quest choices (eg. "extra" options for DB)
Major Stabilizers should outweigh minor volatility - saving the world is more important than stealing a sweetroll.

### Relational state
These tiers represent how much of herself she entrusts to the player.
1. Tier 0 (0-20): Professional
2. Tier 1 (21-40): Respect
3. Tier 2 (41-60): Trust
4. Tier 3 (61-80): Emotional Opening
5. Tier 4 (81-100): Commitment

If Stability = 1 Strategic Liability:

She reverts to / cannot progress past Tier 1-adjacent content.

You can earn respect for competence, but she will not reach Trust.
Emotional Opening is locked.
Commitment is impossible.

if Stability = 2 Controlled Unknown:

She can reach Tier 2 (Trust), possibly Tier 3 if narrative choices are handled well, but she remains cautious. Even at Tier 3, there is analytic distance. Her vulnerability feels deliberate, not surrendered.

If the player reaches Tier 3 and then becomes volatile, she should not revert to Tier 0.
Instead:

Relational Tier remains 3,
Stability drops to Strategic Liability,
Result: Emotional Access Restricted.

She doesn’t forget intimacy.
She withdraws from it.

But here’s the crucial nuance: *Tier 4 does not eliminate her philosophical distrust of concentrated power.* It only means she believes **proximity** is **survivable.**

Events drive Stability heavily.
Dialogue drives Affinity heavily.
But both can nudge the other.

Player is stable but emotionally distant:
High Stability, Low Affinity.

She respects you but does not open.

Player is emotionally supportive but destabilizing:
High AffinityScore, Low StabilityTier (clamped).

She likes you — but won’t risk herself.

**Stability: mostly behavioral telemetry**
Stability answers:
*“Is proximity to this person systemically survivable? How much damage do they do to the scaffolding?”*
Dialogue should not massively change this.

At most, dialogue can:
Add small stability nudges when player explicitly endorses destabilization
Add small positives when player articulates restraint or institutional logic

But if someone talks like a bureaucrat and then slaughters guards, events win.

You don’t want Stability to be pure telemetry because she’s testing belief models too.

If a player repeatedly chooses dialogue options like:
- “Power justifies itself.”
- “Rules are for the weak.”
- “Institutions deserve to fall.”
Even without committing crimes, that’s ideological destabilization.

So dialogue can adjust StabilityScore modestly.
Not big swings. Just trend nudges.

Likewise, if the player consistently:

- Advocates distributed power
- Supports procedural solutions
- Avoids centralizing themself around the player

That should gently increase Stability.

But again — small numbers.

Events should outweigh rhetoric.

**Affinity: mostly relational interaction**
Primary drivers:

- Handling her vulnerability
- Engaging her ideas
- Not dismissing her analysis
- Not exploiting her position
- Encouraging her agency

Affinity answers:
*“How much of myself can I safely entrust to this person?”*

Affinity should not be immune to events.

If someone repeatedly destabilizes cities, her relational openness should slow down even if they say the “right” lines.
So events don’t directly subtract affinity much — they clamp it.

If actions and dialogue conflict: Actions win.

If dialogue and actions align: Growth accelerates.

## Failure States

### Soft Failure:
**(Relational Stall)**

Player remains in Affinity Tier 0–1 (maybe low 2).

Caused primarily by:
- Dismissive dialogue
- Consistent intellectual belittling
- Treating her as a tool
- Mishandling vulnerability repeatedly

This does not make her leave - It makes her stay professional.

This is narratively satisfying because:

She remains competent.
She remains observant.
She simply never entrusts *herself.*

No drama.
No explosion.
Just distance.

And importantly — **this is recoverable for most of the game.**

**Until Minor Mission 2. Minor Mission 2 locks out Tier 4 permanently, and if a player hasn't gotten to 40+ Affinity, she will take the offer and leave.**

### Hard Failure:
**(Strategic Withdrawal)**

Triggered by Stability, not Affinity.

She leaves quietly.
A sigh.
A brief spell.
A gate shimmer.
Gone.
No confrontation.
No fight.
No emotional meltdown.
If affinity >= 3, maybe one line as she steps through the gate: “...This is no longer sustainable.” (3) or “I have made a grievous error.” (4)

That fits her character perfectly because leaving loudly would be *unsafe.*

Now here’s the important distinction:

**Hard failure should not trigger from one spike.**

It should require:

Sustained Liability state over time.

Not:
- One guard kill
- One jail visit
- One Daedric choice

But:
- Pattern of systemic erosion.
- Feeling like her own life is in danger

There should be warning signals before the final break; a three-layer escalation:

**Layer 1: Disapproval Signals**
Triggered when StabilityScore dips into Liability band but not catastrophic.

She:
- Comments on inelegance.
- Uses colder phrasing.
- Mentions exposure, risk, attention.
No mechanical change yet.

Example comments: "This draws attention."

**Layer 2: Containment Warning**
Triggered when sustained Liability persists.

She:
- Explicitly frames player as escalating.
- Pulls back emotionally.
- Reduces personal disclosure.
- Possibly warns once: “This is going to read wrong." / "This is becoming difficult to justify."
- Deeper warnings: "I cannot account for this."
Still no disappearance.

(Optional / variant for very high Affinity + Liability — the crack)
"...You are making this very difficult."

**Allowing recovery:**
If player:
- Stops destabilizing behavior for extended time
- Completes major stabilizing quest
- Pays bounty
- Avoids cities during beast/vampiric feeding
StabilityScore drifts upward, containment Warning fades.

**Layer 3: Break Threshold**
Triggered when:

All Stability-triggered warnings have fired
AND A qualifying destabilizing event just occurred

That last condition is key - the break should piggyback on an event.

Example triggers:
- Guard killed in a city
- High bounty spike
- Jail release
- Repeated assault in populated cell
Not passive drift, so she reacts in real time to the transgression event that confirms the pattern.

We are not designing:
*“When does the player fail?”*

We are designing:
*“When does she conclude that continued proximity is irrational?”*

# How do we track this?

**Dialogue branches:**
As a nod to Bioware/Fallout 4, in dialogue with Alenawe we will present sub-branches of topics, gated by conditions.
- *"Yes, I respect and argee with your position."* (+X Affinity)
- *"Insert sarcastic/chaotic response here."* (mod Affinity)
- *"That's stupid, I just want to hurt them"* (-X Affinity)

Minor relational shift: ±5
Moderate: ±8
Major vulnerability mishandled: ±12–15 (rare)

Conversations are not weighted equally:
Conversation 1 (early respect-building):
Low weight. ±5 max.

Conversation 2–3 (intellectual probing):
Moderate weight. ±8.

Conversation 4 (first vulnerability):
High weight. ±12.

Conversation 5 (major ideological confrontation):
Very high weight. ±12–15.

MinM2 relational decision:
Binary flag + moderate affinity adjustment.

Example:

6 conversations × average ±8 = 48 potential shift.
Add maybe 2 high-impact moments at ±12.

Enough to differentiate but not enough to permanently doom someone for two awkward responses.

Two major emotional missteps should not permanently lock Tier 3.
Three major missteps probably should.

If someone mishandles Conversation 2 badly (-12), they should still be able to recover in Conversation 4 or 5.

That means:
- No single dialogue choice should subtract more than 15.
- Total possible negative accumulation should not exceed recoverable range.

You do not want:
- One wrong line → no romance → reload save.

You want:
- Pattern of behavior → outcome.

Tier 3 locked if:

EffectiveAffinity < 61 at time of trigger.

Tier 4 locked if:

EffectiveAffinity < 81
OR

StabilityTier = 1 (Strategic Liability)
OR

Minor Mission 2 agency choice negative.

But importantly:

No permanent hard lock until MinM2 resolution.
- Before that, players can recover.
- After MinM2, commitment outcome is set.

That preserves stakes without early punishment.

if average player:

- Handles most dialogue neutrally or well
- Doesn’t consistently belittle her
- Doesn’t destabilize systems heavily
- They’ll probably land around 70–85 by endgame.

That means:
Tier 2 → almost everyone
Tier 3 → most
Tier 4 → only those who handled vulnerability + MinM2 correctly

# Mechanics
How is this implemented in-game?

Stability is tracked from game start (like elligibility). It is not *evaulated* until Mission_Begins.
Early negative spikes should decay.

Event-based triggers for major volatility.

Use:
- OnActorKilled (guard faction)
- OnBountyChange
- OnCrimeGoldIncrease
- OnEnterJail
- OnVampireFeed if target = guard
- OnAssault in populated cell

These events:
Immediately adjust StabilityScore.

But break logic should not fire directly from score change.

When a destabilizing event occurs:

Update StabilityScore.
Check if StabilityScore < CriticalThreshold.
If yes AND not already in WarningState:
If previously in ContainmentWarning → break.
Else → escalate to Warning.

### StabilityScore (range: -100 to +100)

Major Positive Stability (big boosts, rare events)
+30 MQ completion
+20 Dawnguard completion
+20 Dragonborn completion
+15 Civil War resolution
+10 Major Daedric threat contained (selected quests)

Minor Positive Stability
+2 Paying bounty
+1 Serving jail time (capitulation signal; does not erase conviction cost)
+3 Thane status gained
+1 Books read threshold milestone
+5 Long stretch of no crime

Major Negative Stability
-20 Guard murder
-25 High bounty threshold
Jail conviction record is a net negative when combined with the compliance signal above
-10 Civilian murder
-15 Repeated city combat incidents

Minor Negative Stability
-2 Assault
-3 Bounty spike small
-5 Repeated intimidation abuse (if you track it)

StabilityScore should slowly drift toward 0 over time when no major events occur (avoids early-game lockouts). Every X in-game hours without major volatility:
StabilityScore moves 1–2 points toward 0.
Major Stabilizers override history (institutional memory).

StabilityTier (derived from StabilityScore)
StabilityScore ≤ -25 → Tier 1 (Strategic Liability)
-25 < StabilityScore < +25 → Tier 2 (Unknown Variable)
≥ +25 → Tier 3 (Stable Asset)

### AffinityScore (range: 0 to 100)

Starts at 0.

Use escalating deltas:

Early convo: ±8–10
Mid convo: ±12–15
Late vulnerability: +18 / -12
MQ2 choice: +20 / -15

Allow Affinity to decrease.

Do not allow it below 0.
