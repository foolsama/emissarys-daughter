## Pre-mission dismissal

One-line summary:   
Player greets Alenawe or Alonythil before gaining enough renown to be their mission target, and is dismissed/rebuffed/ignored.

Background:   
Alenawe has been assigned to Falkreath to observe how a minor Jarl operates and governs, and how well Thalmor doctrine is being followed/received. She is undercover as a scholar of governance, so her Thalmor studies are not polluted by direct observation.   
She is assigned to operate under the supervision of Alonythil, a middle-aged Thalmor spy. He is undercover as her doctoral advisor. He is tasked with making sure Alenawe can operate well in the field, and is furthering Thalmor objectives to the letter.  
Note: Neither of these two are Justicars \- their goal is observational, not correctional. Alenawe is watching the locals, Alonythil is watching Alenawe.

Scene:

If the player greets Alonythil, he will only have a dismissal line:

	**ALONYTHIL**  
	I’m very busy right now.

If the player greets Alenawe;

	**PLAYER**  
Hello, there.

Alenawe turns to the player, a questioning look on her face, studying the player a beat longer than is comfortable. Alonythil moves to intercept. Both are mildly curious and polite, confident. The player is of no importance to them, but being rude or condescending would be counter-productive.

	**ALONYTHIL**  
*(To Alenawe, while facing the Player)*  
Is this individual relevant?

	**ALENAWE**  
*(A beat)*  
Undetermined.

	**ALONYTHIL**  
Then we remain on task.

	**ALONYTHIL**  
	*(Walking away, addressing Alenawe as if she will follow)*  
What is your analysis of the local succession so far?

Alenawe continues to observe the Player, discerning, and follows the mentor after several beats.

If the player attempts to speak with Alonythil again, he will offer only one dismissal, and only once.

	**ALONYTHIL**  
	*(Slightly colder, but still polite)*  
	Conversation is unwarranted.

If the player attempts to greet Alenawe again, she may offer only one line of dismissal.

	**ALENAWE**  
	*(Confident, polite, neutral)*  
	If a discussion becomes relevant, I will find you.

## Mission Begins Handoff

Summary:  The player now has enough renown that the Thalmor are observing them. The player has spoken with Alenawe, and presented an opportunity to observe closer (as a follower). Alenawe is reporting this to her supervisor.

Alenawe walks over to Alonythil, who is busy with report work. She waits silently for his attention.

	**ALONYTHIL**  
	(Closes his work after several beats)  
	You’ve reached a conclusion.

	**ALENAWE**  
	An opportunity has presented itself.

	(They both turn to look at the player for a long beat)

	**ALONYTHIL**  
	Proceed.  
	(Pause)  
	Your judgement will be evaluated.

	**ALENAWE**  
	(Walking back toward the player)  
	Understood.

	**ALONYTHIL**  
	(Stares with hardened eyes at Alenawe’s retreating form and then goes back to work)

## Late (Post-DI) Recruitment
Summary: The player recruits her after having already completed Diplomatic Immunity. Never witnesses Handoff scene.
The minimum explanation could simply be:
	Her original governance posting has already been overtaken by the Embassy crisis.
	Her analysis of the Dragonborn has been requested.
	Direct access to the subject is an opportunity she chooses to exploit.
	Whether that choice was explicitly authorized can remain ambiguous until later. 
her decision to approach an “active threat” alone is already evidence that she is becoming more operational—and more willing to act before permission arrives.
Setup: Add Mission Briefing to her inventory. Its contents might establish:
	The Embassy perpetrator is classified as an active threat.
	Existing reports are materially inconsistent regarding motive.
	If safe access becomes possible, Alenawe should obtain a direct behavioral assessment.
	Apprehension is expressly prohibited.
	Disclosure of operational affiliation is not authorized.
	Continued proximity beyond initial assessment requires review.

That last point is useful. The player who steals the note learns that Alenawe recruited herself into a situation she had not actually been authorized to enter.
