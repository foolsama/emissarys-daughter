# Arc Summaries

## The cover

| Beat | Cover Status | What the Player Sees |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Recruitment | Intact (`"I'm researching Nordic ruins"`) | Nothing openly contradicts the story. |
| Minor Quest 1 | Cracking | Insider language slips through before she corrects herself. |
| Major Quest 1 | Transparent | Her contacts, methods, and procedural fluency no longer read like a simple researcher cover. |
| Interlude | Personal | The player sees that the institutional story and the family story are intertwined. |
| Minor Quest 2 | Gone | Alonythil speaks to her openly in front of the player. |
| Major Quest 2 | Reframed | The question is no longer who she is, but what she will do with that truth. |

The cover peels rather than breaks:
- `MinQ1`: the player hears her think like an insider.
- `MajQ1`: the player sees her operate like one.
- `Interlude`: the player understands why the institutional stakes are personal.
- `MinQ2`: concealment is no longer the point.
- `MajQ2`: identity is settled; alignment is not.

The game never has to force the player to confront her cover directly. The player may notice, may ask, or may remain silent. That silence is meaningful.

## Shadow Path

If the player dismisses Alenawe after the cover has meaningfully cracked, the arc does not end. It changes shape.

The purpose of the Shadow Path is to show that:
- she continues functioning without the player,
- the institution persists without the player's permission,
- and dismissal is information, not a content failure state.

Narratively, the Shadow Path preserves the arc's core question under colder conditions. It turns proximity into absence and asks whether trust can be rebuilt once the fiction is gone.

## Possible played orders

### Path 1 — Ideal (`MinM1 → MajQ1 → DI`)

The intended order. `MinM1` plants the question, `MajQ1` tests it, and `DI` breaks the remaining confidence in the old model.

Late recruitment carries more tension, and the early missions must do more work more quickly. The arc compresses, but remains legible if recruitment establishes stress clearly.

### Path 2 — `MinM1` only before `DI`, `MajQ1` never fires

`DI` becomes the first major model failure. The arc still holds because `MinM1`, `Interlude`, `MinM2`, and `MajQ2` can carry the thematic spine without the first major test.

### Path 3 - DI happens before Recruitment

`Interlude` fires off first. `MinM1` and `MajQ1` never fire, we move straight to `MinM2`, then `MajQ2`.

# Arc with "Who Am I" Central Spine

## Recruitment

**Purpose:** Establish the cover, the attraction of the player as a variable, and the first impression of Alenawe as useful, controlled, and more observant than she appears.

Normal recruitment frames the player as an opportunity. Post-`DI` recruitment frames the player as a problem she needs to understand quickly.

## Minor Quest 1 — Observation That Slips

**Purpose:** Let the player hear Alenawe think for herself before she intends to.

This is not a formal quest so much as an advanced character introduction. Its job is to create asymmetry:
- she reveals a thought she normally keeps internal,
- the player notices,
- she notices being noticed.

Thematically, `MinQ1` introduces:
- institutional function versus lived reality,
- the difference between procedural legitimacy and demonstrated capability,
- emotional neutrality as habit rather than truth.

The player-facing question is simple: how does someone trained inside structure react to a person who keeps solving problems without needing that structure?

Its narrative value is not resolution. It plants the fracture.

## Major Quest 1 — Model Failure In The Field

**Purpose:** Move the question from conversation into action.

If `MinQ1` asks how the player operates without scaffolding, `MajQ1` shows Alenawe a case where her framework and the player's approach no longer produce equivalent results.

This quest exists to prove that:
- her institutional model is competent,
- competence is not always enough,
- and the player can solve problems in ways her framework cannot meaningfully contain.

The player does not "teach" her here. The player forces comparison. She leaves the quest recalculating, not conceding.

## Interlude — When The Lie Fails To Protect

**Purpose:** Turn institutional tension into personal tension.

The Interlude is where protocol fails as insulation. It does not make her defect. It makes neutrality stop feeling safe.

This beat exists to show that:
- the institution does not protect itself cleanly,
- authority can become emotionally compromised,
- and Alenawe's inherited systems of trust no longer align with events.

If `MajQ1` is operational strain, the Interlude is relational strain. It shifts the central question from what she believes to who she is inside the system that taught her to believe it.

## Minor Quest 2 — The Stakes

**Needs journal entry.**

**Purpose:** Make the conflict explicit: the system wants to reclaim her, and she has to answer.

`MinQ2` exists because the earlier missions raise the question of whether she can remain where she is. This quest is where the institution asks the same question in its own language.

Its narrative function is to:
- formalize the cost of staying with the player,
- reveal what Alonythil can and cannot say inside the system,
- and force Alenawe to articulate what she has become.

This is not rebellion as spectacle. It is refusal as self-definition.

## Major Quest 2 — Choosing A Frame That Holds

**Purpose:** Test whether the position she argued for in `MinQ2` can survive contact with reality.

If `MajQ1` tests her framework against the player, `MajQ2` tests her chosen alignment against the institution.

The quest exists to answer a later-stage question:
- can she still use institutional language honestly,
- can she collaborate with the player without reducing them to a variable,
- or does she finally choose honesty over legibility?

By this point the arc is no longer about noticing cracks. It is about choosing what kind of structure, if any, can still hold.

## Path meanings at the end of the arc

### Path A — Coherence inside structure

She remains within an institutional frame, trying to make it usable without fully escaping it.

### Path B — Coherence inside inevitability

She stops pretending the player can be contained and aligns herself with the reality that power has already outrun the system.

### Path C — Coherence through synthesis

She tries to build a new frame that can hold both institutional intelligence and the player's operational illegibility.

## Why this arc exists

At the highest level, the arc is here to ask:
- what institutions are actually selecting for,
- what happens when correctness and reality diverge,
- and whether a person trained to remain legible can choose a self-authored frame instead.

Each quest advances that question in a different way:
- `Recruitment` introduces the variable.
- `MinQ1` lets the player hear the crack.
- `MajQ1` forces comparison.
- `Interlude` makes the stakes personal.
- `MinQ2` demands articulation.
- `MajQ2` demands choice.

That is the spine the player should feel, even if they never read the machinery underneath it.
